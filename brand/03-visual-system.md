# OUTBAKES — Visual System v1

> Working document. Layer 4 of: Brand Core → Brand Architecture → Verbal System → **Visual System** → 3 Strategic Applications → Website.
> Principle: **Precision × Warmth** — the warmth of baking, the discipline of performance.
> Public layer. Derived to carry the verbal grammar in Layer 3.

---

## 0. The core idea

Warmth and precision are not blended into a compromise; they are held in **tension**. Two material
families do the work:

- **Structure / discipline** — Carbon, Graphite, Ivory. Engineered, neutral, exact.
- **Thermal result / warmth** — Baked Gold, Perfect Crust, Soft Wheat. The deep golden hue of
  transformation: Maillard, caramelization, the perfect crust.

The warm tones signal **the correct result of heat applied well** — not the heat source. This is why
the palette is *caramel/crust*, not *ember/fire*: OUTBAKES points at the outcome, not the flame.

---

## 1. Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| OUTBAKES Carbon | `#171816` | Primary dark ground; wordmark on light |
| Precision Graphite | `#272925` | Secondary surface; panels, dividers, UI depth |
| Warm Ivory | `#F4F0E7` | Primary light ground; primary text on dark |
| Soft Wheat | `#D8C5A5` | Warm neutral; large fields, secondary surfaces |
| Baked Gold | `#B97932` | **Signal color** — accents, highlights, key marks |
| Perfect Crust | `#8F5528` | Deep warm; type on light, rich accents, borders |

### Balance (the anti-bakery ratio)
- **70%** structure — Carbon / Ivory / Graphite
- **20%** warm neutrals — Soft Wheat and mid warm tones
- **10%** signal — Baked Gold / Perfect Crust

Baked Gold is a **signal, not a background.** The moment gold becomes the dominant field, we read as
a generic bakery. Keep it scarce and it reads as precision.

### Functional accent (outside the brand ratio)
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Active Ember | `#DD4B1A` | **Interface only** — active-heat / optimum-state indicators in hardware & Intelligence UIs |

The narrative: **Caramel = brand warmth. Ember = active heat.** Ember never appears as a brand fill,
logo color, or marketing background — only as a live functional state. This makes color part of the
proposition, not decoration.

---

## 2. Contrast & pairing (practical)

| Foreground | On | Use | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm Ivory `#F4F0E7` | Carbon `#171816` | Primary text, dark surfaces | ~15:1 — excellent |
| Carbon `#171816` | Warm Ivory `#F4F0E7` | Primary text, light surfaces | ~15:1 — excellent |
| Baked Gold `#B97932` | Carbon `#171816` | Accents, display, UI marks | ~5:1 — fine for large/UI; ok for body, verify per size |
| Perfect Crust `#8F5528` | Warm Ivory / Soft Wheat | Text & accents on light | good on light **only** |
| Soft Wheat `#D8C5A5` | Carbon / Graphite | Light-on-dark text, large fields | dark grounds only |

Avoid: Perfect Crust or Soft Wheat as **text on Carbon** (dark-on-dark, too low). Gold-on-gold or
crust-on-wheat for text (insufficient separation).

---

## 3. Light & dark

Both modes are first-class; the brand is not "a dark brand" or "a light brand," it is a *precise* one.

- **Dark surface** (hardware, Intelligence UI, hero moments): Carbon ground, Ivory text, Graphite
  panels, Baked Gold signal, Ember for live states.
- **Light surface** (Kitchen/consumer, editorial, dossier): Ivory ground, Carbon text, Soft Wheat
  fields, Perfect Crust accents, Baked Gold signal.

The warm family is what keeps the *dark* mode from reading cold-tech, and the structure family is
what keeps the *light* mode from reading rustic.

---

## 4. Typography (v1 direction — candidates, not locked)

Two roles, mirroring the two material families:

- **Precision workhorse** — a neutral, engineered grotesque for almost everything (nav, body, UI,
  spec, labels). Candidates: Suisse Int'l / Neue Haas Grotesk / Söhne (licensed) · Inter / IBM Plex
  Sans (open). Tight, exact, low-drama.
- **Warm register** — used sparingly for brand and editorial moments only. Either a refined
  high-contrast serif (candidates: GT Sectra / Canela / Reckless) for felt warmth, **or** stay
  all-sans and let color + photography carry warmth. Decide at lockup stage.

Rules: the workhorse dominates; the warm face is a guest, never the host. No script, no rounded,
no "hand-made" faces — those pull to bakery cliché.

---

## 5. Wordmark logic

- **OUTBAKES** set as an all-caps wordmark in the precision workhorse: even weight, deliberate
  positive letter-spacing, exact. Reads engineered before it reads warm.
- Permanent lockup with the master line beneath, quiet and small:
  > **OUTBAKES**
  > Built to Outbake.
- If a single warmth cue is introduced (e.g., one letter or the baseline carrying a subtle
  Baked-Gold-to-Crust transition), it is used **once** and restrained — the visual equivalent of the
  verbal scarcity rule. Never gradient the whole mark.
- One wordmark, applied everywhere. No descriptor (Pro, Home, Intelligence) gets its own logo.

---

## 6. Imagery & texture

The hero subject is **the result**, not the process romanticised.

- **Yes:** macro of a perfect crust; Maillard surfaces; the sheen of correct caramelization;
  precision hardware under controlled studio light; clean, legible interfaces; steam and heat shown
  as *controlled*, not chaotic.
- **No:** flour clouds, aprons, rolling pins, rustic wood, brown kraft, chalkboard menus,
  "with love" styling, hands-in-dough nostalgia.

Lighting is directional and disciplined; warmth comes from the *subject's* color (crust, gold), not
from a warm filter over everything.

---

## 7. Motion (brief)

- Restrained, precise easing; nothing bouncy or playful.
- Where heat/transformation is expressed, it is a **slow, controlled** shift (a surface reaching its
  gold), and Active Ember appears only to mark a live/optimum state, then settles.

---

## 8. Across the three demonstration territories

One system, three inflections — structure constant, warm family dialed to context.

- **Systems (hardware):** Carbon-dominant, Ivory + Graphite, Baked Gold as the single signal;
  Active Ember for live heat states. Most engineered.
- **Intelligence (software):** Carbon/Graphite UI, Ivory text, Baked Gold for key data/accents,
  Active Ember strictly for optimum-state / alerts. Precise, calm, legible.
- **Kitchen (consumer):** more Ivory + Soft Wheat, Perfect Crust and Baked Gold accents, Carbon for
  type and structure so it never drifts into generic bakery. Warmest, still disciplined.

---

## 9. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Keep 70% structure / 20% warm / 10% signal | Let Baked Gold become the background |
| Use Ember only as a live functional state | Use Ember as a brand or marketing color |
| Show the *result* (crust, Maillard) | Show flour dust, aprons, kraft, rustic wood |
| Let the workhorse type dominate | Lead with a decorative/script face |
| Introduce a warmth cue once, restrained | Gradient the whole wordmark |
