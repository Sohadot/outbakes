# OUTBAKES — Visual System

Principle: **Precision × Warmth** — the warmth of baking, the discipline of performance. Warmth and
precision are held in tension, not blended into a compromise, through two material families:

- **Structure / discipline** — Carbon, Graphite, Ivory. Engineered, neutral, exact.
- **Thermal result / warmth** — Baked Gold, Perfect Crust, Soft Wheat. The deep golden hue of
  transformation: Maillard, caramelization, the perfect crust.

The warm tones signal *the correct result of heat applied well* — not the heat source. The palette
is caramel/crust, never ember/fire: OUTBAKES points at the outcome, not the flame.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| OUTBAKES Carbon | `#171816` | Primary dark ground; wordmark on light |
| Precision Graphite | `#272925` | Secondary surface; panels, dividers, depth |
| Warm Ivory | `#F4F0E7` | Primary light ground; primary text on dark |
| Soft Wheat | `#D8C5A5` | Warm neutral; large fields, secondary surfaces |
| Baked Gold | `#B97932` | Signal colour — accents, highlights, key marks |
| Perfect Crust | `#8F5528` | Deep warm; type on light, rich accents, borders |

**Balance:** 70% structure (Carbon / Ivory / Graphite) · 20% warm neutrals · 10% signal
(Baked Gold / Crust). Baked Gold is a signal, never a background — kept scarce, it reads as
precision; as a field, it reads as a generic bakery.

**Functional accent (outside the brand ratio):** Active Ember `#DD4B1A` — interface only, for
active-heat / optimum-state indicators. Never a brand fill, logo colour, or marketing background.
Caramel = brand warmth; ember = active heat.

## Contrast & pairing

- Warm Ivory on Carbon / Carbon on Warm Ivory — primary text, excellent contrast.
- Baked Gold on Carbon — accents, display, UI marks.
- Perfect Crust / Soft Wheat on light grounds only — never as text on Carbon (dark-on-dark).

## Light & dark

Both modes are first-class. Dark surfaces: Carbon ground, Ivory text, Graphite panels, Baked Gold
signal, Ember for live states. Light surfaces: Ivory ground, Carbon text, Soft Wheat fields, Perfect
Crust accents, Baked Gold signal. The warm family keeps dark mode from reading cold-tech; the
structure family keeps light mode from reading rustic.

## Typography

- **Precision workhorse — Space Grotesk.** Nav, body, UI, spec, labels, and the wordmark. Tight,
  exact, low-drama.
- **Warm accent — Newsreader italic.** Used rarely, for a single editorial/sensory moment, and only
  in the Kitchen register. Never in Systems or Intelligence.

The workhorse dominates; the warm face is a guest. No script, rounded, or hand-made faces.

## Wordmark

OUTBAKES set in the workhorse, all caps, even weight, deliberate positive letter-spacing — reads
engineered before it reads warm. Permanent lockup with the master line beneath, quiet and small. One
wordmark, applied everywhere; no descriptor takes its own logo.

## Imagery

The hero subject is the **result**, not the process romanticised.

- Yes: a perfect crust; Maillard surfaces; the sheen of correct caramelization; precision hardware
  under controlled light; clean, legible interfaces; heat shown as controlled.
- No: flour clouds, aprons, rolling pins, rustic wood, brown kraft, "with love" styling.

Lighting is directional and disciplined; warmth comes from the subject's own colour, not a filter.

## Motion

Restrained, precise easing. Where heat/transformation is expressed, it is a slow, controlled shift;
Active Ember appears only to mark a live/optimum state, then settles.

## Across the three territories

One system, three inflections. **Systems** — Carbon-dominant, most engineered, Baked Gold signal,
Ember for live heat. **Intelligence** — Carbon/Graphite UI, Ivory text, Baked Gold for key data,
Ember only for optimum-state. **Kitchen** — more Ivory and Soft Wheat, Perfect Crust and Baked Gold
accents, Carbon type so it never drifts into generic bakery. Warmest, still disciplined.
