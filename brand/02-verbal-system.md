# OUTBAKES — Verbal System

The brand's defining property is that its name behaves as a verb: *to outbake* — to surpass in
baking. This system governs how OUTBAKES speaks, how the name conjugates, and what it never does.
The verb must always feel inevitable and rare — never clever for its own sake.

## The four grammatical states

Each form has one job; they are not interchangeable.

**`OUTBAKES` — the mark.** Identity. All caps, standalone, never possessive. Pairs permanently with
the master line:
> **OUTBAKES**
> Built to Outbake.

**`Outbake` — the imperative.** A call to act, for campaigns and CTAs. Sentence-case, directed at
the reader:
> Outbake the expected. · Outbake inconsistency. · Outbake yesterday.

**`Outbakes` — the third-person proof.** Lets any product present itself by doing the verb — the most
portable line in the system:
> It Outbakes.

**`Outbaking` — the continuous.** Occasional texture in body copy only. Never a headline, a lockup,
or the basis of a line.

## Prime directive: demonstrate the verb, never explain it

On every primary surface the brand performs the verb and lets the reader arrive at the meaning:
> This oven doesn't just bake. It Outbakes.

The dictionary sense may appear once, quietly, as evidence — never as an explanation on a primary
surface.

## Line hierarchy

The tiers are deliberately unequal; that inequality is what keeps the verb from becoming a gimmick.

| Tier | Line | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Master line | **Built to Outbake.** | Always-on; pairs with the mark. |
| Product proof line | **It Outbakes.** | Stable, reusable — a demonstration moment. |
| Campaign territory | *Outbake the expected.* · *Better baking doesn't happen by accident.* · *Precision you can taste.* | Variable, time-bound. |

The master line commits only to the **result**, not to what we outperform with — which is why it can
sit above hardware, intelligence, and ingredients at once.

## The scarcity rule

The verb is a spice, not the meal. The mark and master line carry the wordplay; on any single
surface it appears at most once more (typically the proof line). Everything else is plain, sensory,
precise. A brand that puns once, perfectly, is confident.

## Voice

**Confident · sensory · precise · restrained · performance-led.** The technical register serves
baking; it never colonises it.

Two poles to avoid:

| Claim-heavy hype | Bakery cliché |
|---|---|
| Revolutionary. Disruptive. | Made with love. |
| Category-defining. | From our kitchen to yours. |
| World's most advanced. | Freshly baked with a smile. |

Done right:
> Heat, timing and judgment change the result. OUTBAKES is built around making that result better.

## The masterbrand test

Every core line must pass before adoption. Could an **ovens** company, an **ingredients** company,
and a **baking-intelligence** platform each say it truthfully? Passes all three → core language.
Passes one or two → product copy, kept out of the core. *Built to Outbake.* and *It Outbakes.* pass
all three.

## Voice across the three territories

One voice, three inflections — the subject changes, not the personality.

- **Systems (hardware):** control and repeatability. *Control the variables. Improve the result.*
- **Intelligence (software):** foresight. *Know what changes the bake before the bake changes.*
- **Kitchen (consumer):** sensory, precise, never twee. *Precision you can taste.*

## Lexicon

**Use:** result · outcome · heat · timing · judgment · precision · control · consistency ·
performance · better · repeatable · variables.

**Avoid:** revolutionary · disruptive · game-changing · category-defining · world's most · rustic ·
"with love" · magic · secret recipe.
