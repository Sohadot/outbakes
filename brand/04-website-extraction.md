# OUTBAKES — Website Extraction (Layer 5.5)

> Transitional layer: extract the site's language FROM the three applications, rather than invent a
> second language for the site. Everything here is lifted from Layers 3–4 and the Layer 5 mockups.
> Public layer.

---

## Governing rules (read first)

1. **OUTBAKES is the core identity; each territory is a manifestation of it.** The site's default skin
   is **dark (Systems-derived)**. Systems and Intelligence set the master tone; Kitchen is contained.
2. **Kitchen containment.** Kitchen appears *inside* the dark system as one proof, never as the site's
   overall warmth. Test at final review: *is Kitchen still speaking OUTBAKES, or an independent
   consumer brand?* If the latter — dial warmth back (less ivory bleed, smaller serif moment).
3. **Verb scarcity.** `Built to Outbake.` is always-on. `It Outbakes.` appears **at most twice** on the
   whole page, at genuine moments of demonstration. Never a repeating rhythm.
4. **No defensive language.** No category claims, no domain comparisons, no explanation of why the
   word is good. The brand behaves with confidence; the visitor concludes.

---

## 1. Hero logic
- **Primary scene: Systems** (precision oven, controlled heat, the Ember optimum state). Highest
  strategic value, most engineered — it anchors the brand as *performance*, not product.
- **Not a composite.** One committed scene, dark. A composite would dilute the anchor.
- Hero carries only: the wordmark, the master line, one short support line, and a quiet scroll cue.

## 2. UI chrome (constant across the page)
- **Wordmark:** top-left, `OUTBAKES`, Space Grotesk 600, letter-spacing ~6px, Warm Ivory on dark.
- **Territory / system labels:** uppercase, ~11px, letter-spacing ~3px, Soft Wheat at ~62% — top-right
  of a scene or above a section.
- **Captions:** ~10px, letter-spacing ~2px, Soft Wheat muted.
- **Hairlines / dividers:** 1px. On dark: `#2f312b`. On warm/light: `#3a3c35`. Sections separate by
  hairline + space, not heavy blocks.
- **Disclaimers:** on any mockup-derived scene — *"Brand vision concept — not a currently marketed
  product."* ~10px, muted.

## 3. Type roles
- **Headline:** Space Grotesk 600, tight negative tracking (`-0.5px`), large.
- **System label:** Space Grotesk, uppercase, positive letter-spacing.
- **Body / support:** Space Grotesk 400, generous line-height, sensory + precise.
- **Warm accent:** Newsreader italic — **rare**, and reserved. Not in Systems or Intelligence.
  At most one warm serif moment on the page, echoing Kitchen. (This is a type-level expression of
  the Kitchen-containment rule.)

## 4. Image / scene behavior
- **Backgrounds default to dark Carbon.** Ivory is used deliberately and sparingly — a single warm
  panel (the Kitchen proof), not the page ground.
- **Scenes are isolated on a field** — one subject, controlled light, generous negative space. The
  *result* (crust, controlled heat, precision hardware, a calm interface), never process romance
  (flour dust, aprons, kraft).
- **Ember appears only as a functional state** inside a Systems/Intelligence scene (optimum reached).
  Never decoration, never a brand fill.

## 5. Copy hierarchy
- **Master line** — `Built to Outbake.` Always-on: hero and the acquisition/footer sign-off.
- **Support line** — sensory, precise sentences that commit to the *result* (pass the ovens /
  ingredients / intelligence test).
- **Proof line** — `It Outbakes.` Scarce (≤2 on the page), at demonstration moments only.
- **Disclaimers** — concept labels on mockup-derived scenes; acquisition legal kept minimal.
